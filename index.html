Patient notes

Questions

Beforeyou book

Is the first consultation free?

It costs $95 and takes an hour, with a 3D scan and photos. If you go ahead with treatment, the $95 comes off your first bill.

Do you work with insurance?

We are not tied to any insurer. You pay us directly and get an itemised receipt with treatment codes, which most insurers accept for reimbursement.

Can I spread the cost?

Yes. Treatment over $1,500 can be paid in up to 12 monthly instalments with no interest. We set this up at the planning visit.

I'm nervous about dentists. What can you do?

Tell us when you book. We plan a longer first visit, explain each step before we do it and agree on a stop signal. Sedation is available for implant surgery.

How long do veneers last?

With good care, porcelain veneers usually last 10 to 15 years. We check them at every hygiene visit and repair chips while they are small.

What happens after I send the form?

We reply within one working day with two or three times that suit you. Nothing is booked until you confirm one of them.
